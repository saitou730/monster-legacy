Authority: `docs/WORK_GITHUB_MASTER_HANDOFF_2026-09-07.md`.

2. No text-only regeneration of existing characters. SOURCE → identify/crop → freeze DNA → source-referenced derivative → visual comparison QA → PASS → LOCK.
3. Frozen: face, apparent age, hair, eye read, build, species/form, silhouette, palette, costume architecture, signature ornament, weapon class, countable anatomy. Pose/camera/expression/background/lighting/VFX/layout/polish may change within those limits.
