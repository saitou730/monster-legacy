## Visual Direction Lock
HOME is a compact frontier rest/work space belonging to the Chapter 1 AREA loop, not a grand town illustration and not a lore-heavy hero painting.

Composition for Portrait 9:16:
- Upper 20%: subdued wall/roof/storage shapes; low contrast for header/title overlays.
- Middle 45%: readable open staging zone for current party/interaction cards; no permanent character painted into background.
- Lower 35%: visually quiet floor/table edge with enough negative space for thumb-reach navigation and modal UI.
- Depth: shallow-to-medium. HOME must feel safer than FIELD without becoming bright, glossy, luxurious, or visually busy.

Material / palette:
- weathered timber, muted stone, cloth, restrained warm practical light;
- neutral earth/gray base with limited warm accents;
- no faction emblem, unexplained crest, giant monument, religious symbol, neon/magical-tech wall decoration, or story-specific NPC portrait;
- avoid uniform AI gloss, excessive micro-detail, symmetrical prop clutter, and high-frequency highlights.

Reuse:
- PARTY uses the same HOME base; runtime/UI may crop/grade/defocus it rather than request another raster.
- TITLE/PROLOGUE are not a reason to fork this HOME asset.
